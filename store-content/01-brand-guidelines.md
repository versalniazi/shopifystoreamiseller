## Brand Personality
Professional · Modern · Luxury · Trustworthy · Minimal · Clean · Corporate · High-Converting · Premium

**In practice, this means:**
- Short, confident sentences. No hype, no exclamation-mark stacking.
- Calm authority over salesy pressure — we sound like a firm executives already trust, not a discount service.
- Every claim is either a stated fact about the service (delivery time, revisions, format) or clearly framed as typical/illustrative (testimonials, samples).
- Whitespace is a feature. Never crowd sections.

## Voice & Tone
- **We say:** "Your resume should open doors, not get filtered out." **Not:** "OMG your resume is probably getting rejected!!"
- **We say:** "3 business days." **Not:** "Lightning fast!!"
- Second person ("you," "your career") throughout marketing copy. First person plural ("we," "our writers") for the company voice.
- Avoid clichés common to competitors: "land your dream job," "stand out from the crowd" — used sparingly if at all, replaced with concrete, specific language (ATS pass rate context, recruiter behavior, keyword matching).
- Sentence case for most UI labels; Title Case for nav items and buttons.

## Typography
- **Headings:** A modern serif-adjacent or geometric sans with a premium feel — recommended: **"Fraunces"** (display serif, for H1/hero only, optional) paired with **"General Sans"** or **"Inter"** for all other headings. If limiting to one family for simplicity/performance, use **Inter** or **Sohne**-style geometric sans throughout, weight 600–700 for headings.
- **Body:** **Inter** or **IBM Plex Sans**, weight 400, 16–18px base, 1.6 line-height.
- **Scale:** H1 44–56px / H2 32–36px / H3 22–24px / Body 16–18px / Small 14px.
- Use tabular figures for pricing.

## UI Language
- **Cards:** rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`), 1px `#E2E8F0` border, soft shadow (`0 4px 20px rgba(15,23,42,0.06)`), extra shadow + slight lift on hover.
- **Buttons:** rounded-full or 12px radius, solid blue `#2563EB` primary, outline navy secondary, teal used only for small accent buttons/badges (e.g., "Most Popular").
- **Icons:** professional line-icon set only (e.g., Lucide/Phosphor "line" style) — no filled emoji-style icons, no clip-art.
- **Imagery:** editorial, muted, professional — no stock-photo clichés (no thumbs-up handshakes). See `16-image-icon-suggestions.md`.
- **Motion:** subtle fade/slide-in on scroll, 200–300ms, no bounce/elastic easing — premium brands move calmly.
