# 12. Trust Elements

Implemented via the `trust-badges` section (theme-wide) and the announcement bar. Only claims that are operationally true or clearly framed as illustrative are used — no invented client counts or unverifiable "as seen in" logos.

| Badge | Where it appears | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| Secure Checkout | Footer, product pages, trust-badges section | "Secure Checkout" (accurate — Shopify's PCI-compliant checkout) |
| Worldwide Delivery | Announcement bar, footer, product pages | "Worldwide Clients" / "Digital Delivery Worldwide" |
| Fast Delivery | Trust-badges section | "Fast Delivery" (backed by stated per-package business-day windows) |
| Professional Writers | Trust-badges section | "Professional Writers" |
| ATS Optimized | Announcement bar, ATS section, product pages | "100% ATS-Optimized" |
| Email Support | Package inclusions (Executive/Ultimate) | "30-Day Email Support" |
| Premium Quality | Brand positioning throughout | Reinforced by design system, not a standalone badge claim |
| Confidential Service | Footer, trust-badges, Privacy Policy | "100% Confidential" |

**Money-back guarantee:** deliberately **not** presented as an unconditional badge — see `08-policies/refund-policy.md`. Trust is built instead through a clearly stated, fair Refund Policy and Revision Policy rather than an over-promise that could create false expectations or legal exposure.

## Additional recommended trust signals (add once available)
- Verified client reviews (replace illustrative testimonials in `05-pages/success-stories.md`)
- A real business address / phone number on the Contact page (currently placeholders)
- Payment method icons at checkout footer (Shopify displays these automatically based on enabled gateways)
- An "As featured in" or industry-association row, only once genuinely earned
