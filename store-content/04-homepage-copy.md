## ATS Section
**H2:** What Is an ATS — and Why It Decides If You Get Seen
Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to scan, sort, and rank resumes before a human ever opens one. A resume with the wrong format or missing keywords can be filtered out automatically — regardless of how qualified you are. We build every resume to be read cleanly by ATS software and by the recruiter who reviews it next.

**Checklist:** Clean ATS-readable formatting · Keywords matched to target role · Standard section headings · Consistent formatting · Delivered in Word + PDF
