## Intro
**H1:** Your LinkedIn Profile Is Being Searched. Make It Findable.
Recruiters search LinkedIn using keywords, the same way employers scan resumes with ATS software. We rewrite every section of your profile to be specific, keyword-aligned, and written in a voice that sounds like you.

## What We Optimize
- **Headline** — keyword-rich, beyond your job title
- **About Section** — first-person summary working key search terms in naturally
- **Experience Rewrite** — each role rewritten around outcomes
- **Skills Optimization** — reordered and refined to match what's actually searched
- **Keyword Optimization** — industry/role-specific keywords woven throughout
- **Profile Review** — photo guidance, banner, featured section, completeness

## Personal Branding
Your resume, cover letter, and LinkedIn profile should all sound like the same professional. LinkedIn Optimization aligns with your resume's positioning for a consistent, credible narrative.
