# Cover Letter — Page Copy

Implemented in `shopify-theme/templates/page.cover-letter.json`.

## Intro
**H1:** The Case Your Resume Doesn't Make on Its Own
A resume shows what you've done. A cover letter explains why it matters for this specific role — and why you're the one to hire. We write cover letters that read like a confident, specific pitch, not a restated resume.

## Why Cover Letters Matter (Benefits)
- Directly connects your background to the specific role and company
- Gives context a resume can't — career changes, gaps, relocation, framed on your terms
- Demonstrates communication ability, itself a job requirement for most roles
- Signals genuine interest over a generic, mass-sent application

## Structure
1. **Opening Hook** — specific, confident opening naming the role and strongest qualification
2. **Proof of Fit** — 2-3 concrete examples connecting experience to employer needs
3. **Why This Company** — a specific, non-generic reason for targeting this employer
4. **Confident Close** — clear call to action

## ATS & Formatting
Many ATS systems accept and parse cover letters alongside resumes. We use clean formatting and align language with your target job description, same as our ATS resumes.
