## Intro
**H1:** A Resume Built Around What You've Actually Achieved
Most resumes list job duties. Ours are built around outcomes — the specific, measurable results that show a hiring manager exactly what you're capable of. Every resume we write is structured for two readers at once: the applicant tracking system that scans it first, and the human recruiter who decides what happens next.

## Resume Types We Write
- **Professional Resume** — A clean, achievement-based resume for mid-level professionals — the foundation of every package.
- **ATS Resume** — Formatted specifically to parse cleanly through applicant tracking systems, with keyword alignment to your target job postings.
- **Modern Resume** — A contemporary layout with clear visual hierarchy — professional, not flashy, and still fully ATS-safe.
- **Executive Resume** — Built for director-level and above: leadership impact, P&L ownership, and strategic results, not task lists.
- **Keyword-Optimized Resume** — Keywords pulled from real job descriptions in your target field.
- **Interview-Focused Resume** — Structured so every bullet gives you a natural, confident talking point at interview.

## Benefits
- Pass automated screening instead of being filtered out before a human sees your application
- Communicate your value in the first 6-10 seconds a recruiter spends scanning
- Consistent, error-free formatting across every application
- Reuse a strong foundation across multiple tailored versions for different roles
