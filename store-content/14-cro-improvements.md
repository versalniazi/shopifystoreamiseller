# 14. CRO Improvements

## Implemented in this build
- **CTA after every major homepage section** (hero, how-it-works, ATS section, industries, closing banner) and at the end of every service/page template.
- **Sticky mobile CTA bar** (`snippets/sticky-cta.liquid`) — persistent purchase path on the device where most traffic likely lands.
- **Social proof stacked early**: star rating + proof line in the hero, stats bar directly below, testimonials before the FAQ.
- **"Most Popular" and "Premium Choice" badges** on the Job Hunter Bundle and Ultimate Career Transformation pricing cards — a documented, effective anchor for guiding undecided buyers toward the packages with the best margin/value balance.
- **Before/After resume framing** (`resume-samples` section) — makes the abstract value of "professional writing" concrete.
- **Process timeline** (`how-it-works`) reduces purchase anxiety by showing exactly what happens after checkout, before the visitor commits.
- **Benefits-vs-features balance**: every pricing card lists concrete inclusions; every service page pairs a features list with a benefits section that translates those features into outcomes for the reader.
- **FAQ objection-handling**: refund/ATS/guarantee questions are answered directly on the pricing/product pages themselves, not only on a separate FAQ page — reduces last-minute cart abandonment from unanswered doubts.

## Recommended next steps (not automatable without merchant input)
- **Add a post-purchase intake form** (Typeform/Google Form embed or a Shopify order-status page block) so customers submit their resume/job target immediately instead of waiting for an email reply — shortens delivery clock and reduces support back-and-forth.
- **A/B test the hero CTA copy** ("View Packages" vs. "Get Your Free Resume Review" as a lower-commitment first step) once traffic volume supports testing.
- **Add exit-intent capture** only if it can be implemented tastefully (a subtle top-of-page banner offering a free ATS checklist download in exchange for email) — avoid aggressive full-screen exit popups given the premium brand positioning.
- **Introduce a low-commitment lead magnet** (e.g., a free downloadable "ATS Resume Checklist" PDF) tied to the newsletter signup to build an email list before someone is ready to buy.
- **Display real client review counts and star ratings** once the Shopify Product Reviews app is installed and reviews accumulate — replace illustrative testimonials with a review count that compounds credibility over time.
- **Segment packages by "quiz" on entry** (a 2-question "What's your career stage?" selector at the top of the Packages page) to route visitors to the right price tier faster — reduces choice overload across 8 packages.
- **Add urgency only if true** (e.g., "X writer slots available this week" tied to real capacity) — do not use fake countdown timers or fabricated scarcity, which undermines the "Trustworthy" brand pillar explicitly requested.
