# 11. Blog Content Plan — "Career Advice"

**Blog name:** Career Advice
**Blog handle:** `career-advice`
**Purpose:** SEO-driven content marketing supporting Hire Nestt Limited's resume, cover letter, and LinkedIn packages. Tone follows brand guidelines: professional, modern, calm, no hype, no invented statistics, no promises of job offers or interview outcomes — articles position Hire Nestt as a knowledgeable, trustworthy resource, with natural internal links to relevant packages (e.g., an ATS article links to the Starter Career Package or Professional Resume; an executive article links to the Executive Career Package).

Category tags used below are drawn from: `ATS`, `Resume Tips`, `Cover Letters`, `LinkedIn`, `Interview Prep`, `Career Growth`, `Industry Guides`, `Executive Careers`, `Graduate Careers`.

Total entries: **44**

## Content Calendar Notes
- Publish roughly 2 articles per week to build a steady content cadence without overwhelming editorial resources.
- Each article should include a short, non-hype call-to-action linking to the most relevant package (e.g., ATS articles → Starter Career Package or Professional Resume; executive articles → Executive Career Package; graduate articles → Entry-Level Graduate Package; LinkedIn articles → LinkedIn Optimization or Job Hunter Bundle).
- Avoid duplicating exact keyword phrases across articles within the same group to reduce internal keyword cannibalization.
- All articles should maintain brand voice: factual, calm, specific — no invented statistics, no guarantees of interviews or job offers.
