## ATS & Keyword Optimization

### What is ATS, and why does it matter?
ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System — software that many employers use to collect, sort, and search resumes before a human ever reviews them. A resume that isn't formatted or worded with ATS in mind can be harder for these systems to parse correctly, which is why formatting and keyword alignment are treated as core parts of every Hire Nestt package.

### Which packages include ATS optimization?
ATS-conscious formatting and keyword alignment are built into every resume package, from the Entry-Level Graduate Package through the Ultimate Career Transformation. The Starter Career Package ($65) is specifically structured as an ATS review and optimization service for an existing resume rather than a full rewrite.

### How does the ATS review process work for the Starter Career Package?
For the Starter Career Package, you submit your existing resume and we review its structure, formatting, and keyword usage against ATS best practices, then return an optimized version within 2 business days with one revision included. This is a lighter-touch service than a full rewrite, intended for applicants who have a working resume that needs refinement rather than a rebuild.

### Do you guarantee my resume will pass every ATS?
No. ATS platforms vary widely between employers, and no resume writer can guarantee how a specific system will parse a specific document. What Hire Nestt provides is formatting and keyword practices aligned with widely used ATS conventions — not a guarantee of outcome with any individual employer's software.

### How do you choose which keywords to include?
Keywords are drawn from the job titles, industries, and target roles you specify when submitting your information, along with the content of your existing resume and career background. We do not insert generic or irrelevant keywords purely to "game" a system — every keyword included is meant to reflect a genuine skill or experience area.

## Revisions

### How many revisions come with each package?
Revisions vary by package: the Entry-Level Graduate Package and Starter Career Package each include 1 revision, Professional Resume includes 2 revisions, and Resume + Cover Letter, LinkedIn Optimization, and the Job Hunter Bundle each include 3 revisions. The Executive Career Package and Ultimate Career Transformation include unlimited revisions for 30 days from delivery.

### What counts as a "revision"?
A revision is a round of edits to the delivered document(s) based on your feedback — for example, adjusting wording, reordering sections, refining a summary, or incorporating a role you'd like added. Revisions are meant to refine and correct the delivered draft, not to restart the project around an entirely different job target or industry.

### What happens once I've used all my included revisions?
Once your revision allowance is used, any further changes can typically be arranged for an additional fee, which we will quote based on the scope of the requested changes. This is why we encourage reviewing your draft carefully and submitting all requested edits together where possible.
