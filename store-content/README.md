## Important note on personal content
Muhammad Zohaib's LinkedIn profile (linkedin.com/in/muhammad-zohaib48) could not be accessed during research — it returned a login/access-restricted response and no public record could verify its contents. Per the original instruction not to invent professional experience, certifications, or years of experience, **the About Us page and all copy deliberately avoid unverified personal claims** about the owner. If you'd like a personal bio added, paste the verified LinkedIn About/Experience/Certifications text into a follow-up request and it can be incorporated into `05-pages/about-us.md` and `shopify-theme/templates/page.about-us.json`.
