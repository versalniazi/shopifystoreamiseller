This Cookie Policy explains how Hire Nestt Limited ("Hire Nestt," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains more broadly how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function properly, to improve the user experience, and to provide information to the website owner about how visitors use the site. Similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, and local storage, may be used alongside cookies and are covered by this Policy.

## 2. Categories of Cookies We Use

**Essential / Checkout Cookies.** These cookies are strictly necessary for our website and checkout process to function. They enable core functionality such as maintaining your shopping cart, securing checkout, remembering your session, and preventing fraud. Our store is built on the Shopify platform, which sets certain essential cookies automatically to enable browsing, cart, and checkout functionality. These cookies cannot be switched off without affecting the ability to purchase from our store.

**Analytics Cookies.** Where enabled, we use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website, such as which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and which packages are most viewed. This helps us improve our website content, layout, and overall customer experience. Analytics data is generally aggregated and does not directly identify you.

**Marketing and Retargeting Cookies.** Where enabled, we or our third-party partners may use marketing cookies to deliver advertisements relevant to your interests, measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns, and show you relevant content on other websites and social media platforms after you have visited our store. These cookies track your browsing activity across websites and are only used where permitted under applicable law and, where required, with your consent.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are placed by third parties rather than by Hire Nestt directly, including:

- **Shopify**, our e-commerce platform provider, which sets cookies necessary for site functionality, cart management, and checkout security.
- **Analytics providers** (such as website analytics tools), which help us measure site traffic and performance.
- **Advertising and social media platforms**, where enabled, which may set cookies to support retargeting and marketing campaigns.

These third parties are responsible for their own cookie practices, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy and cookie policies for further information.

## 4. How to Control Cookies

You have several options for managing or limiting cookies:

- **Cookie consent banner.** Where a cookie consent banner is displayed on our website, you can use it to accept or decline non-essential cookie categories, and to update your preferences at any time.
- **Browser settings.** Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking all cookies, blocking third-party cookies only, or deleting cookies that have already been set. Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our website, including completing a purchase.
- **Opt-out tools.** For advertising cookies specifically, you may also be able to opt out through industry opt-out platforms or the settings provided directly by advertising networks.

Because browser and device settings vary, please refer to your browser's help documentation for specific instructions on managing cookies.
