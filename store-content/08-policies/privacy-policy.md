# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: [Effective Date]**

Hire Nestt Limited ("Hire Nestt," "we," "us," or "our") provides professional resume writing, cover letter writing, LinkedIn profile optimization, and related career-services packages delivered digitally to clients worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the personal information you share with us when you visit our website, place an order, or otherwise interact with our services. By using our website or purchasing our services, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following categories of information:

- **Contact and account information:** your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and billing/shipping details entered at checkout.
- **Payment information:** payment card details or other payment information are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Shopify Payments (and any other payment gateway enabled on our store). We do not store full payment card numbers on our own servers.
- **Career and service-related information:** your existing resume or CV, LinkedIn profile content, employment history, education, certifications, job target details, target industries, and any other career information you voluntarily submit so that our writers can complete your order.
- **Communications:** emails, order notes, and messages you send to us, including revision requests and feedback.
- **Technical and usage data:** IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing behavior on our website, collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies as described in our separate Cookie Policy.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Process and fulfill your order, including preparing and delivering your resume, cover letter, LinkedIn content, or other purchased deliverables by email.
- Communicate with you about your order status, request additional information needed to complete your service, and respond to support or revision requests.
- Verify payment, prevent fraud, and comply with our obligations to our payment processor.
- Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
- Send you service-related updates and, where you have consented, marketing communications about our offerings. You may opt out of marketing emails at any time.
- Comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored using Shopify's hosting infrastructure and, where applicable, secure email and file-storage systems used by our writing team. We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Uploaded resumes, CVs, and career documents are used solely for the purpose of completing your order and are retained only as long as necessary as described in Section 6 below.

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share limited personal information with trusted third-party service providers strictly as needed to operate our business, including:

- **Shopify**, which hosts our online store and provides e-commerce infrastructure.
- **Shopify Payments** (or other enabled payment gateways), which processes payment transactions on our behalf.
- **Email service providers**, used to deliver order confirmations, communications, and final deliverables.
- **Analytics and marketing tools** (where enabled), used to understand website traffic and improve our services, as further described in our Cookie Policy.

These providers are only permitted to use your information as necessary to provide services to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own independent marketing purposes.

## 5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate the checkout process, remember your preferences, and, where enabled, measure site performance and marketing effectiveness. Full details on the categories of cookies we use and how to control them are set out in our separate Cookie Policy, which forms part of this Privacy Policy by reference.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information, including uploaded career documents, only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill your order, provide any applicable revisions, respond to support requests, and comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Once these purposes have been fulfilled and any applicable retention period has expired, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize your information.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for individuals in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents. These rights may include the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal or contractual exceptions.
- Object to or restrict certain processing of your information.
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (Hire Nestt does not sell personal information).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10. We will respond to verified requests within the time frame required by applicable law.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to, and are not intended for use by, individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly.

As our customers are located worldwide and our service providers may operate in different countries, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.
