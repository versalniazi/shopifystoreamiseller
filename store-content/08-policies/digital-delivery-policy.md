# Shipping & Delivery Policy (Digital Delivery)

**Effective Date: [Effective Date]**

Hire Nestt Limited ("Hire Nestt," "we," "us," or "our") provides 100% digital career-services packages. This policy explains how your order is delivered, since no physical products are shipped and no courier tracking applies to any purchase made through our store.

## 1. Digital-Only Service

All services sold by Hire Nestt, including resume writing, cover letter writing, LinkedIn profile optimization, and bundled career packages, are delivered entirely by email in digital file format. We do not ship any physical items, and there is no postal delivery, courier, or tracking number associated with any order. References to "delivery" throughout our store and policies refer exclusively to the electronic delivery of your completed files.

## 2. How Delivery Works

Our delivery process follows these steps:

1. **Order placed.** You select and purchase your chosen package through our online store using our secure checkout.
2. **Confirmation email.** Immediately after purchase, you will receive an automated order confirmation email to the address provided at checkout, confirming your order details and package selection.
3. **Information request.** Our team will follow up by email requesting the information needed to begin your project, including your current resume or CV, relevant career history, and your job target details (target role, industry, and any specific preferences). Work on your order begins once we have received this information.
4. **Drafting.** One of our professional writers prepares your deliverable within the business-day turnaround window associated with your purchased package, as set out in Section 3 below.
5. **Final delivery.** Once complete, your final files are emailed directly to you. Depending on your package, this includes your resume and/or cover letter in both Word (.docx) and PDF format, and, where applicable, a separate LinkedIn profile content document.

## 3. Delivery Timeframes by Package

Turnaround times are measured in business days, beginning once we have received all necessary information from you to start your project. The applicable delivery windows for each package are:

- **Entry-Level Graduate Package** — 3 business days
- **Starter Career Package** — 2 business days
- **Professional Resume** — 3 business days
- **Resume + Cover Letter** — 4 business days
- **Job Hunter Bundle** — 5 business days
- **LinkedIn Optimization** — 5 business days
- **Executive Career Package** — 5 business days
- **Ultimate Career Transformation** — 5 to 7 business days

These timeframes represent our standard turnaround under normal working conditions and may occasionally vary during periods of high demand or public holidays; if any delay is expected, we will notify you by email.

## 4. Business Days

"Business days" refers to Monday through Friday and excludes weekends and public holidays observed by our team. An order placed on a Friday evening or over a weekend will typically begin processing on the next business day. Turnaround windows do not include weekends or holidays in the count of days.

## 5. What to Do If You Do Not Receive Your Delivery Email

If you do not receive your confirmation email, information request, or final delivery email within the expected timeframe, please take the following steps:

1. Check your spam, junk, or promotions folder, as automated emails are occasionally filtered by email providers.
2. Confirm that the email address entered at checkout was correct and free of typos.
3. If you still cannot locate the email, contact us directly at hirenest56@gmail.com with your order number so we can resend your files or investigate the issue promptly.

## 6. No Physical Shipping Charges

Because all deliverables are provided digitally, no shipping fees, customs charges, or import duties apply to any order placed with Hire Nestt, regardless of your location. Our services are available to customers worldwide, and delivery is made electronically to your provided email address in all cases.
