# Revision Policy

**Effective Date: [Effective Date]**

Hire Nestt Limited ("Hire Nestt," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to ensuring your final deliverable accurately reflects your career history and presentation preferences. This Revision Policy explains how many revisions are included with each package, what qualifies as a revision, how to request one, and the applicable time windows and turnaround.

## 1. Revisions Included Per Package

Each package includes a set number of complimentary revisions, as follows:

- **Entry-Level Graduate Package** — 1 revision
- **Starter Career Package** — 1 revision
- **Professional Resume** — 2 revisions
- **Resume + Cover Letter** — 3 revisions
- **Job Hunter Bundle** — 3 revisions
- **LinkedIn Optimization** — 3 revisions
- **Executive Career Package** — unlimited revisions for 30 days from delivery
- **Ultimate Career Transformation** — unlimited revisions for 30 days from delivery

Revisions included with your package are provided at no additional cost within the applicable request window described in Section 4.

## 2. What Counts as a Revision

A revision covers reasonable adjustments to the deliverable already prepared for you, based on the same job target and career history originally submitted. This includes, for example:

- Wording, tone, or phrasing adjustments.
- Formatting, layout, or design changes.
- Adding, removing, or updating specific roles, achievements, dates, or details you did not initially include.
- Correcting errors or omissions in the information as originally provided.
- Minor adjustments to better align the document with a specific job posting within the same general target role or industry.

## 3. What Counts as a New Order

The following are considered outside the scope of a revision and would instead require a new order or an additional paid service:

- A full rewrite of your resume, cover letter, or LinkedIn content for a materially different job target, career path, or industry than originally specified, requested after final delivery.
- A request to apply completed revisions to an entirely new document not covered by your original order.
- Additional packages or services not included in your original purchase (for example, adding a LinkedIn Optimization service to an order that only included a resume).

If you are unsure whether your request falls under a revision or a new order, contact us at hirenest56@gmail.com and we will clarify before any work begins.

## 4. Revision Request Window

- **Standard packages** (Entry-Level Graduate, Starter Career, Professional Resume, Resume + Cover Letter, Job Hunter Bundle, LinkedIn Optimization): revision requests must be submitted within 14 days of final delivery.
- **Premium packages** (Executive Career Package, Ultimate Career Transformation): unlimited revisions are available within 30 days of final delivery.

Once the applicable window has closed, further changes will be treated as a new order and quoted accordingly.

## 5. How to Request a Revision

To request a revision, email hirenest56@gmail.com with:

- Your order number.
- The specific changes you would like made, described as clearly as possible (for example, exact wording changes, sections to add or remove, or formatting preferences).
- Any updated information needed to complete the change, such as a newly added role or certification.

Clear, specific requests allow our writers to complete your revision accurately and efficiently on the first pass.

## 6. Turnaround Time Per Revision

Standard revision requests are typically completed within 1 to 2 business days of receipt. More extensive revision requests, or those submitted during periods of high demand, may take slightly longer; in such cases, we will notify you of the expected turnaround.
