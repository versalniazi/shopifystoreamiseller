# Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date: [Effective Date]**

This Cancellation Policy explains when and how you may cancel an order placed with Hire Nestt Limited ("Hire Nestt," "we," "us," or "our"), and what happens to your payment in each case. Because our services involve custom professional writing prepared specifically for each client, cancellation terms are tied to the stage of work completed at the time of your request.

## 1. When You Can Cancel

**Before drafting begins.** You may cancel your order at any time before our writing team has begun drafting your deliverable. As a general guideline, cancellation requests made within 24 hours of purchase, and before drafting has started, are accepted without complication. If you are uncertain whether drafting has begun on your order, contact us and we will confirm the current status before processing your request.

**After drafting has started.** Once a writer has begun working on your project, the order can still be cancelled, but it is no longer eligible for a full refund, since professional time has already been committed. See Section 3 below for how payment is handled in this case.

**After final delivery.** Once your completed files have been emailed to you, your order is considered fulfilled and cannot be cancelled. At this stage, our Revision Policy applies if changes are needed, rather than cancellation or refund.

## 2. How to Request a Cancellation

To cancel an order, email hirenest56@gmail.com as soon as possible with:

- Your order number.
- The email address used at checkout.
- A brief statement that you wish to cancel your order.

We recommend submitting cancellation requests as early as possible, since the stage of work in progress at the time we receive your request determines the outcome under this policy.

## 3. What Happens to Your Payment

- **Work not yet started:** If drafting has not begun at the time we receive your cancellation request, you will receive a full refund to your original payment method.
- **Work in progress:** If drafting has already started, a partial refund may apply, reflecting the portion of work completed, consistent with our Refund Policy. Our team will assess the stage of completion and confirm the applicable partial refund amount before processing.
- **Final files delivered:** If your final deliverable has already been sent, the order is not eligible for cancellation or refund, and our Revision Policy applies instead should you need adjustments to the delivered materials.

Approved refunds arising from a cancellation follow the same processing timeframe described in our Refund Policy, generally 5 to 10 business days back to your original payment method.

## 4. Order Changes Short of Cancellation

If you do not wish to cancel your order entirely but need to change details such as your job target, target industry, or specific instructions before drafting begins, please contact us as early as possible. We will do our best to accommodate changes at no additional cost when requested before work has started.
