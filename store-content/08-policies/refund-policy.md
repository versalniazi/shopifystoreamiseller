# Refund Policy

**Effective Date: [Effective Date]**

Hire Nestt Limited ("Hire Nestt," "we," "us," or "our") provides professionally written, custom career-service deliverables, including resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn profile content, and related packages. Because each order involves original creative and professional writing work prepared specifically for you, our refund policy is structured around the stage of work completed at the time a refund is requested. This policy is designed to be fair to both our clients and our writing team. Please read it carefully before placing an order.

## 1. Before Drafting Begins

If you request a refund within 24 hours of purchase, and our writing team has not yet begun drafting your deliverable, you are eligible for a full refund of the amount paid. To confirm whether drafting has begun, please contact us at hirenest56@gmail.com with your order number as soon as possible after purchase.

## 2. Once Drafting Has Started

Once a writer has begun working on your order, we are no longer able to offer a full refund, because time and professional effort have already been committed to your project. In this case, a partial refund may be issued, reflecting the stage of work completed at the time of the request. The exact amount will be assessed on a case-by-case basis by our team and communicated to you before any refund is processed.

## 3. After Final Delivery

Once your final deliverable (Word document, PDF, and/or LinkedIn content, as applicable to your package) has been completed and emailed to you, the order is considered fulfilled and is no longer eligible for a refund. At this stage, if adjustments are needed, our Revision Policy applies instead of a refund. Revisions allow you to request wording, formatting, or content changes within the applicable revision window for your package at no additional cost, up to the number of revisions included with your purchase.

## 4. No Refunds for Change of Mind After Delivery

Because our services involve custom, personalized work product created specifically for each client, we do not offer refunds for change of mind, dissatisfaction with subjective stylistic preferences after final delivery, or decisions not to use the delivered materials, once the final files have been sent and any included revisions have been made available. We encourage clients to review our sample work and package descriptions carefully before ordering, and to use the revision process to align the final deliverable with their expectations.

## 5. Exceptions: Duplicate Charges and Technical Errors

Regardless of the stage of your order, you are entitled to a full refund in the following circumstances:

- You were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical or processing error.
- A payment error resulted in an incorrect charge amount.
- You paid for a service that was never delivered due to a fault on our end, and no alternative resolution is possible.

Please report any billing discrepancy to hirenest56@gmail.com as soon as you notice it, along with your order number and a description of the issue, so we can investigate and correct it promptly.

## 6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email hirenest56@gmail.com with:

- Your order number.
- The email address used at checkout.
- A brief explanation of the reason for your request.

Our team will review your request, confirm the current stage of your order, and respond within a reasonable timeframe to advise on eligibility and next steps.

## 7. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are issued to your original method of payment. Please allow 5 to 10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer's own processing times. Hire Nestt is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions once a refund has been issued on our end.

## 8. Non-Transferable and Non-Cash Alternatives

Refunds, where approved, are issued to the original payment method only and cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, or transferred to a different order or third party.
