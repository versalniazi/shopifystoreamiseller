## Which package should you choose?

**Fresh graduate.** If you're a student or recent graduate with limited full-time work experience, start with the **Entry-Level Graduate Package ($55)**. It's built around education, coursework, and early achievements rather than a long job history, so it presents what you do have in the strongest possible structure. If you already have a first draft that isn't landing interviews the way you expected, the **Starter Career Package ($65)** can audit and fix it directly instead.

**Mid-level professional.** If you're a few years into your career and need a resume that reflects your actual scope of work, the **Professional Resume ($70)** is the standard starting point. If you're actively applying and want your cover letter aligned with it, move up to **Resume + Cover Letter ($110)**. If you're applying broadly and want your LinkedIn profile working alongside your resume, the **Job Hunter Bundle ($199)** covers resume, cover letter, and LinkedIn together at a lower combined cost than buying each separately.

**Executive or senior leader.** For director-level and above, the **Executive Career Package ($299)** is built for the way senior candidates are actually evaluated — leadership scope and impact, not task lists — and includes a 1:1 consultation call plus 30 days of unlimited revisions. If your search is complex (multiple target roles, industries, or geographies) or you want structured interview preparation as part of the process, the **Ultimate Career Transformation ($399)** extends the Executive package with an interview preparation guide, a personal branding statement, and cover letters tailored to more than one job target.

**Career changer.** If you're moving into a new industry or function, positioning matters more than volume of experience. The **Professional Resume ($70)** or **Resume + Cover Letter ($110)** gives your existing experience a clear, transferable framing for a new field. If your search will also depend heavily on networking and recruiter outreach, add LinkedIn Optimization — either standalone at $150 or as part of the **Job Hunter Bundle ($199)** — so your profile supports the same narrative as your resume. Candidates changing careers at a senior level often find the **Executive Career Package ($299)** worthwhile for the consultation call alone, since positioning questions are usually the hardest part of a pivot.
